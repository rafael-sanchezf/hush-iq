Photo-test labeler

Open the eval/photo_test folder to start. Each listing is a folder 01, 02, … with listing.txt, photos/ and labels.json.

New listing: paste the link and the description, then on the listing page right-click a photo, Copy Image, and press ⌘V here, once per photo. Labels: click one answer per field, or Can't tell. Amber fields were pre-filled from the text; any click confirms them. Enter saves and moves on; ← → step through listings.
